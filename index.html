1. Core Privacy Commitment

We believe your personal security and data belong solely to you. AntiTheft Toolkit does NOT sell, monetize, broker, or transfer any user data, photos, GPS coordinates, or emergency contacts to advertisers or third parties.

2. Purpose of Sensitive Telemetry

Intruder Photos

Captured using front camera silently only when wrong unlock credentials are submitted past the limit, or during unauthorized unplug/power-off attempts.

GPS Location

High-accuracy GPS coordinates retrieved during security alarms, failed unlock events, SIM change detection, or remote SMS inquiry.

SIM & IMSI Telephony

Used to detect SIM card removal and notify the emergency contact of the intruder's newly inserted number.

Contacts Backup (.vcf)

Only generated upon remote command to create an emergency vCard backup sent directly to the owner's email.

3. Direct Encrypted Transmission

Alerts are transmitted straight from your device to your personal email address via encrypted TLS/SSL SMTP connections.
