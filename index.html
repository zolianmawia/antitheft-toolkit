1. Introduction & Core Privacy Commitment

This Privacy Policy explains how AntiTheft Toolkit (hereinafter referred to as “the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), developed by independent Android software engineer Zolianmawiia, handles your data and privacy when using our mobile security application on Android devices.

AntiTheft Toolkit is an offline-first physical theft prevention, unauthorized access detection, telemetry alerting, and lost-device recovery suite. Because our primary function is safeguarding your device against theft, unauthorized tampering, and physical intrusion, the App interacts with hardware sensors and system services including the front camera, satellite GPS, telephony state, and SMS broadcast receivers.

Our Commitment: We firmly believe that your personal device and security telemetry belong strictly to you. AntiTheft Toolkit does NOT collect, sell, lease, monetize, share, or broker your personal data, photographs, location coordinates, or contacts to third-party advertisers, analytical services, or data brokers.

2. Architecture: Local On-Device Processing & Zero Cloud Storage

Unlike standard security tools that upload user data to remote commercial cloud servers, AntiTheft Toolkit operates on an on-device client-side architecture:

  • No Remote Company Server: We do not operate or maintain an external central cloud server that logs, stores, or aggregates your photographs, GPS trails, or SIM data.
  • Direct-to-Owner Alert Dispatch: In the event of a security alert (such as an incorrect unlock attempt, SIM ejection, or remote SMS inquiry), all photos, GPS coordinates, and battery telemetry are transmitted directly from your smartphone to your personal email address via encrypted TLS/SSL SMTP connections.
  • Local SQLite/Room Sandbox: All application configuration settings and incident logs are stored strictly inside your phone's private internal storage partition, protected by Android's application isolation framework.

3. Granular Categories of Data Collected & Processed

All data processing performed by the App is strictly dedicated to executing theft deterrence, intruder documentation, and emergency device recovery as explicitly authorized by you:

3.1 Intruder Facial Photographs (Camera Sensor)

Processing Context: The App uses the device's front-facing camera to silently take a photograph when incorrect unlock credentials (PIN, pattern, or password) exceed your chosen threshold (e.g., 3 failed attempts), upon unauthorized device power-off attempts, or when triggered remotely by an authorized SMS recovery command.

Handling: Photographs are cached temporarily in the App's private local internal directory (context.cacheDir) and attached directly to your security email alert. They are never transmitted to third parties.

3.2 Geolocation Data (GPS & Network Triangulation)

Processing Context: High-precision satellite GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, altitude, accuracy) and network cell triangulation are accessed exclusively during active alarm states, intruder captures, unauthorized SIM changes, or upon receipt of a remote SMS location query containing your secret PIN.

Handling: Converted into an interactive Google Maps location link and dispatched immediately via email and emergency SMS to your designated contact.

3.3 SIM Card & Telephony Telemetry (IMSI, Serial, Carrier)

Processing Context: To protect against theft where a perpetrator swaps or removes the SIM card, the App reads the active SIM identity (ICCID/IMSI) upon device reboot or SIM replacement.

Handling: If an unknown or unauthorized SIM is inserted, an emergency SMS is automatically dispatched to your emergency contact revealing the new phone number and device location.

3.4 SMS Remote Security Commands

Processing Context: The App's broadcast receiver listens for incoming SMS messages strictly matching predefined security syntax combined with your master PIN (such as SMS Siren <PIN>, SMS Location <PIN>, SMS Flashlight <PIN>, or SMS Stop <PIN>).

Handling: Standard personal text messages are ignored and untouched. Security command messages are parsed locally on the device to execute the requested action.

3.5 Emergency Contacts & Address Book (vCard Backup)

Processing Context: Your selected emergency contact number and email are stored locally to receive distress messages. If you initiate the remote emergency contact backup feature (SMS Backup <PIN>), the App compiles an industry-standard .vcf vCard file sent exclusively to your personal email address.

3.6 Motion & Hardware Sensors

Processing Context: The hardware accelerometer detects sudden physical movement (Motion Alarm), the proximity sensor detects removal from pockets (Pocket Theft Alarm), and battery state listeners detect unauthorized charger disconnection (Charger Removal Alarm).

4. Android Runtime Permissions & Operational Disclosures

In strict compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies (specifically the Sensitive Data & Permissions Policy), the following list details each permission requested by AntiTheft Toolkit and its singular functional necessity:

android.permission.CAMERA Dangerous / Runtime

Silently captures photos of unauthorized persons attempting to unlock, turn off, or tamper with your lost or stolen device.

android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Dangerous / Runtime

Retrieves accurate GPS coordinates during theft alarms and remote SMS tracking requests.

android.permission.ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION Dangerous / Runtime

Provides fallback cell-tower and Wi-Fi location when satellite GPS signals are unavailable indoors.

android.permission.SEND_SMS Dangerous / Runtime

Sends automated emergency alert SMS messages (with intruder SIM details and GPS links) to your emergency contact.

android.permission.RECEIVE_SMS Dangerous / Runtime

Listens for authorized remote security commands containing your secret master PIN.

android.permission.READ_PHONE_STATE Dangerous / Runtime

Monitors SIM card identity and serial numbers to detect when a thief swaps or removes the device SIM.

android.permission.READ_CONTACTS Dangerous / Runtime

Allows selecting emergency contacts from your phonebook and generates emergency vCard (.vcf) email backups on command.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Dangerous / Runtime

Displays real-time background protection status, foreground service indicators, and incident alerts.

android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE Normal / Install-time

Keeps anti-theft sensors (charger, pocket motion, unlock monitor) continuously active when the screen is locked.

android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Normal / Install-time

Automatically restarts anti-theft protection upon device boot, preventing thieves from silencing alarms via restart.

android.permission.VIBRATE Normal / Install-time

Triggers haptic vibration patterns during high-decibel anti-theft siren activation.

android.permission.INTERNET Normal / Install-time

Transmits encrypted TLS/SSL SMTP security email alerts to your personal email address.

5. Device Administration & Security Protection Capabilities

AntiTheft Toolkit provides an optional Device Administrator capability (DeviceAdminReceiver):

  • Purpose: Android protects lockscreen security events strictly. To detect and count incorrect PIN, pattern, or password attempts and trigger the intruder camera silently, Android requires Device Admin authorization (specifically watch-login).
  • No Remote Factory Reset or Lockout: AntiTheft Toolkit only requests failed-password reporting. The App does not alter your screen lock password, lock out the device, or perform unrequested remote data wipes.
  • User Control: You can enable or revoke Device Admin status at any time directly in the App Settings or via Android System Settings > Security > Device Admin Apps.

6. Direct-to-Email Encrypted Telemetry (SMTP Protocol)

AntiTheft Toolkit provides direct-to-email notifications using standard SMTP (Simple Mail Transfer Protocol) over TLS (Port 587) or SSL (Port 465):

  • All emails (including intruder photos, battery level, and GPS coordinates) are transmitted directly from your handset to your personal destination mailbox.
  • No middleman server or external cloud API intercepts or caches the content of your security notifications.
  • If using Gmail with a Google App Password, the 16-character token remains encrypted exclusively on your local device storage and is never uploaded or shared.

7. Zero Third-Party Advertising & Monetization

To guarantee the utmost data privacy for our users:

  • No Advertising SDKs: AntiTheft Toolkit contains no advertising frameworks (such as AdMob, Unity, or Facebook Audience Network).
  • No Behavioral Tracking: We do not integrate third-party telemetry, behavioral analytics, or user tracking SDKs.
  • No Data Sales: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker user data under any circumstances.

8. Data Retention, Local Storage & Right to Deletion

We respect your right to manage and permanently delete your data:

  • Local Incident Logs: Security incident logs are kept in your device's private SQLite/Room database.
  • One-Tap Log Purge: You can permanently wipe all stored security logs and cached intruder photos at any time by tapping Admin Dashboard > Clear All Logs inside the App.
  • Complete Deletion on Uninstall: Uninstalling the App immediately removes all stored settings, contact records, and incident histories from your device. Because we maintain no external database, there are no lingering cloud copies.
